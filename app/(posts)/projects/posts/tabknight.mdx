Thirty tabs across four windows, and the one you need is somewhere in the blur. TabKnight turns Chrome's tab strip into a fast, keyboard-driven command surface: hit <kbd>⌘K</kbd> and a palette appears over your current page — fuzzy-search every tab in every window, see a real preview of where you're going, and jump there with <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

# Never leave the keyboard

Everything is reachable without the mouse. Arrow through matches, close or pin or mute the highlighted tab with a single chord, type `>` to filter every browser command, or drop into Audio mode to control whichever tab is making noise. The search stays focused even when focus drifts — you just type.

# Sessions that survive

Done with a project? Save the working set of tabs as a bookmark-backed session and restore it later — tomorrow, or on another machine. Your desk gets cleared without losing your place.

# Local-first, by design

Everything lives in your browser. TabKnight makes no network requests of its own, and it degrades gracefully even on Chrome's internal pages. No accounts, no tracking, no server.

# Get it

TabKnight is open source — TypeScript, React 18, and Bun under a Manifest V3 extension. Browse the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/AitorGallardo/tabknight).
