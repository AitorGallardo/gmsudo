If you've ever saved a tweet and never found it again, you already know the problem. X bookmarks are one endless list — no search, no way back to that one post you actually needed.

XSaved fixes that. Install it, open X, and your bookmarks turn into a proper library you can search, sort, and come back to.

Add a quick note to any bookmark — why you saved it, what to do with it later. The context stays with the post, so you never have to remember.

# It's yours, and it stays yours

Everything lives on your own device. There's no account to create and nothing handed off to a server you don't control. Fast, private, and entirely yours — and if you want your library on more than one machine, cloud sync is there as an option, never a requirement.

# How it's built

The extension is local-first: everything lives in the browser's IndexedDB, so there's no server in the loop and nothing to wait on. Full-text search stays instant across tens of thousands of bookmarks by leaning on the right indexes plus a layered cache that reuses work from one keystroke to the next. Optional cloud sync landed in v0.96 for people who want their library on more than one machine.

# One library, every screen

XSaved grew into a family: the extension in your browser, a native iPhone app, and a native Mac app, all backed by one shared sync layer. Save something at your desk and it shows up on your phone on its own.

# Get it

XSaved is live on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/xsaved/nefnhjnjkpimffifpbpdogjoglpjihdn). Add it, open your bookmarks on X, and your library fills in as it syncs. See it in action at [xsaved.com](https://xsaved.com).
