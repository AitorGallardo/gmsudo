# Your folders, everywhere

The folders you've made on X come along for the ride — synced into a sidebar, in the order you left them, and kept in step across the extension, your phone, and the Mac. Organize once, and it holds everywhere.

# How it's built

Native SwiftUI, in TestFlight beta, and it acts as the hub of the shared sync backend the other apps talk to. Folders sync from X in your own order — a folder's position is seeded from X on first import and never re-sorted on later syncs, so nothing you arranged gets shuffled. Bulk folder edits batch into single round-trips with last-write-wins semantics, so reorganizing a lot at once stays fast and predictable.

# On TestFlight now

The XSaved Chrome extension is live today at [xsaved.com](https://xsaved.com), and the Mac app is in TestFlight beta.
