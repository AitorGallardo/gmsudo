# Saved anywhere, here on its own

Save a bookmark from the extension on your laptop and it shows up on your phone by itself — live sync keeps every device on the same page. Save from anywhere in the system with the share sheet, and keep your library one tap away with a home-screen widget.

# How it's built

It's a native SwiftUI app targeting iOS 26, currently in TestFlight beta. It talks to the same sync layer as the extension and the Mac app, so a bookmark saved on another device appears here on its own — no manual refresh. Saving works straight from the system share sheet, and a home-screen widget keeps the library one tap away.

# On TestFlight now

The XSaved Chrome extension is live today at [xsaved.com](https://xsaved.com), and the iPhone app is in TestFlight beta.
